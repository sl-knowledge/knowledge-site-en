# Publication baseline

English is the primary public edition. Chinese may remain the drafting language.

publication-baseline.json records the complete v0.7.0 corpus: 46 pages, including 33 research articles. It records corresponding article versions and normalised text hashes. The research articles were frozen at the Chinese site-v0.6.0 baseline; bilingual introductions and release records were aligned in site-v0.7.0.

This is a provenance snapshot, not a claim of scientific validation. Updating an English public article requires a reviewed version change and a corresponding Chinese update or a recorded temporary discrepancy. The existing source-parity check detects translation drift; it does not make Chinese permanently authoritative, and its separate CI job does not block English publication.

The glossary fixes recurring terminology. pending.json is empty for this release. Future baselines should be recorded explicitly rather than rewriting the historical baseline to conceal a substantive change.
