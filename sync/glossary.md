# Terminology table

This table fixes the English rendering of
the terms that recur across the corpus, so that the two editions use one vocabulary
rather than two drifting ones. Terms marked **fixed** must not be reworded in a
translation; the others may be adjusted for readability only when the fixed sense
would be lost.

Derived from the canonical vocabulary article: [Canonical Vocabulary, Symbols and
Knowledge Levels](content/framework/methods/canonical-vocabulary.md).

## House style

- Keep the English term in the text when the Chinese original keeps an English term,
  for example CF Grammar, Capability, Access, Operational Actionability.
- A term that is fixed above must not be replaced by a synonym in the same edition.
- Parenthetical glosses are allowed on first use only: "reachability (可达性)".
- Do not upgrade a working diagnostic into a law; the hedge belongs to the claim.
- Page titles are translated; the Chinese title is not appended in brackets.
