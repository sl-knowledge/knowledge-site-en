# Versions and research feedback

Articles are available in English and Chinese. Please cite the author, article title, article version, principle or section, and the URL of the edition you read. Site release numbers describe batches of publication and are distinct from article versions.

Corrections, counterexamples and translation reports are welcome through the repository's Issues. Identify the exact statement, relevant assumptions and a supporting argument or source where possible. Reports are reviewed before changing an article.

Definitions, conditional arguments, observations, mechanism hypotheses and design priorities retain their separate status. Safety and investment research retain their research-use boundaries.

Original articles remain under CONTENT-LICENSE.md; Quartz software remains under LICENSE.txt. Third-party materials retain their own rights.
