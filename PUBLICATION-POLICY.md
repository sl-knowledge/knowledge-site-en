# Publication and language policy

The English edition is the primary public edition of SL's research. Chinese remains a drafting language and a corresponding reading edition.

## Versions and interpretation

The present English corpus is translated from a recorded Chinese publication baseline. That records provenance; it does not make every Chinese sentence permanently authoritative. Article versions and principle identifiers are retained in both editions.

Future substantive public revisions are prepared and reviewed in English first, with the corresponding Chinese edition updated and any temporary discrepancy recorded. A language difference is a reason to investigate, not proof that either wording is scientifically correct.

Please cite the author, article title, article version, principle or section, and the URL of the edition you read. Site release numbers describe batches of publication; they are distinct from article versions.

## Research status and corrections

Definitions, conditional arguments, model calculations, observations, mechanism hypotheses and design priorities keep their separate status. Translation does not strengthen a claim or supply missing evidence. Safety and investment research retain their research-use boundaries.

Corrections and counterexamples are welcome through the English repository's Issues. Useful reports identify the exact claim and its assumptions, explain the problem, and provide a derivation, counterexample or source where possible. An issue is a proposal for review; it is not an automatic change to the theory.
