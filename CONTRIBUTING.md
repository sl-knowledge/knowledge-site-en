# Research feedback

The English edition is the primary public research edition. Reports in English or Chinese are welcome.

Before opening an issue, check the article version and existing issues. Please include:

1. Article URL, version, and principle or section.
2. The exact claim or expression being questioned.
3. Whether the concern is a definition, assumption, derivation, empirical claim, translation, reference, or presentation issue.
4. A counterexample, derivation, competing explanation or supporting source, where available.
5. A proposed correction and its effect on related claims.

Distinguish a mathematical error from an unstated modelling assumption, a value disagreement or a claim whose evidence is incomplete. A translation problem can be reported without proposing a theory change.

Do not include private files, credentials or identifying case material. Public reports and replies remain public.

Versioned corrections are reviewed before publication. Opening an issue does not grant permission to relicense the articles or imply that a proposed change has been accepted. See PUBLICATION-POLICY.md and CONTENT-LICENSE.md.
