## What the content here is for

The content on this site is for learning, theoretical exploration, discussion of research methods and analysis of historical cases. It contains working definitions, simplified models, conditional inferences and hypotheses that still await validation. It is not a professional service or a set of execution instructions for a reader's actual tasks.

Phrases such as "should", "needs to", "strategy", "design" and "control" in the articles have to be read in their research context. A research checklist is a list of questions to check; a mathematical conclusion depends on its assumptions; a teaching example cannot be turned directly into a real plan. **That a description can explain a case does not mean it has been shown to guide real operation, improve returns or guarantee safety.**

## Safety, aviation and engineering research

**The safety research published here is not flight guidance, equipment operating instructions, emergency procedures, engineering deployment plans or safety certification.**

Control structures, authority allocations, recovery paths, thresholds, formulas and pseudocode in the articles are objects of research or worked examples, not approved arrangements that apply to any particular device, operating phase or failure scenario. This site has not, through these articles, completed certification, operational approval or deployment validation for any specific system.

None of this replaces applicable current approved documentation, manufacturer material, operating and emergency procedures, professional training or independent engineering review. Do not change flight or equipment operation, take over control, bypass protections, modify hardware or software, or run tests without proper authorisation and risk assessment on the basis of this site alone. Any concrete work must be carried out by people with the relevant qualification and authorisation, following the applicable requirements and procedures.

Citing accident investigations, academic papers or regulatory material does not mean that the institution, manufacturer or author endorses the arrangements described here. The scope, version, investigation status and limitations of cited material still have to be checked separately.

## Investment, trading and company case studies

**The investment and company research published here is not investment, securities, trading or asset-allocation advice, and it is not an instruction to buy, sell, hold, time an entry, size a position, use leverage or exit.**

Companies, securities, industries, valuations, prices and scenarios are mentioned in order to explain research questions. They are not recommendations, target prices, statements of author holdings or promises of returns. This site has not performed a suitability assessment for any reader's financial situation, investment objectives, time horizon or risk tolerance; the articles cannot replace independent due diligence or applicable professional services, and they should not be used as a basis for trading.

Historical cases, simulations, backtests and assumed returns each carry a different evidential weight. Backtests and simulations are not real trading performance; historical results and model estimates do not guarantee future results; a research framework cannot guarantee accurate prediction, preservation of capital or excess returns. Investment can result in loss, including loss of principal, and leverage can amplify losses.

Company operations, valuation assumptions, price formation and personal decisions have to be studied separately. That a company's operations have improved does not follow that its securities are suitable to buy; and that a research judgement looks correct does not follow that any reader is suited to executing a particular strategy.

## Sources, points in time and revision

Every article states its author, version and dates, so that the scope of the material at the time can be traced. The content may contain omissions, errors, outdated information or models that no longer hold. A publication date does not mean that every source in the article was re-verified on that day, and it does not mean that the author undertakes to keep tracking every event.

Research conclusions have to be checked against the original sources, the applicable conditions and the latest material. If you find an error, or a statement that could be misread as an execution instruction, please raise it through the [site repository](https://github.com/sl-knowledge/knowledge-site-en) so that it can be checked and revised.

## What this notice does and does not do

This notice explains the publishing purpose and the boundaries of use for this site. It does not guarantee the reliability of any content, it does not turn unvalidated research into an executable plan, and it is not a guarantee of legal responsibility or compliance status in any jurisdiction.

The body of an article still has to keep a careful, explicit research register. Giving execution advice in the body and closing with a single "for reference only" does not fit the research position of this site.
