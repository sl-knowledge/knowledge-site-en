This site preserves research documents and their continuing revisions. This plan records the next areas of work: clarifying connections between theory, methods and applications, and sustaining checks and revisions.

## Terminology, notation and formulas

- [ ] Use [Canonical Vocabulary, Notation and Knowledge Status](./framework/methods/canonical-vocabulary.md) as a starting point to check the meaning, conditions of use and Chinese and English names of concepts across articles.
- [ ] Distinguish synonymous expressions from different meanings of the same term. Where a symbol is used in different contexts, state the local definitions and their correspondence.
- [ ] Check the assumptions, time scales and conventions behind formulas and derivations, retaining the necessary conditions and statements of knowledge status.

## Links between theory, methods and applications

- [ ] Establish stable entry points for key concepts, linking to existing definition sections where possible.
- [ ] Add links where concepts are used substantively, distinguishing references to definitions, applications of methods and general related reading.
- [ ] Gradually introduce backlinks showing which articles link to the current page, and assess whether separate lists of references to individual concepts are needed.
- [ ] Distinguish concepts from topics and organise topic indexes where useful.

Concept links and backlinks are being tried on a small scale. A link needs to be checked against the meaning in context; identical wording alone does not establish that concepts are the same.

## Alignment between the Chinese and English editions

- [ ] Continue checking corresponding claims, assumptions, terminology, formulas and article versions.
- [ ] Maintain corresponding links between articles and concept entries, reducing shifts in meaning during translation.

## Checks and revisions

- [ ] Check internal links, section anchors, formulas and Markdown rendering article by article.
- [ ] Continue reviewing sources, arguments and knowledge status, distinguishing established results, conditional inferences, hypotheses and unknowns.
- [ ] Keep traceable revisions in response to corrections and counterexamples, and record important changes in the [site update log](./updates.md).

## How the work will proceed

Review a small sample first, then extend the work in batches. A shared terminology document, article versions, corresponding Chinese and English texts and a classified outline are already in place. Each still requires checks against the content itself.

This plan will be revised as the research and organisation develop, without a fixed completion date. Questions about terminology, links and arguments can be submitted through [research feedback](https://github.com/sl-knowledge/knowledge-site-en/issues/new?template=research-feedback.yml).
