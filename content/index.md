## How these notes grow

Start from a model small enough to derive. Turn the derivation into an expectation, then let the difference between reality and that expectation drive the next round of questions.

This site collects public drafts on learning methods, economics, language and writing, theory frameworks and theory applications. The articles are research records under continuous revision; each one states its version and its dates at the top.

Enter the theory framework through the [five drafts](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site/framework/book/), the [research methods and standards](./framework/methods/index.md), [systems, life and values](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site/framework/foundations/) or the [case studies](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site/framework/cases/).

[Theory applications](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site/applications/) collect the models, hypothetical cases and test plans that appear when theory enters a concrete problem.
