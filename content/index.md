## How these notes grow

Start from a small question and try to find the simple reason behind it. Follow that reason down, then check whether reality actually works that way. When something does not add up, go back and keep asking.

Here are notes on learning, economics, language and complex systems. They are added to slowly, and they keep changing as new evidence and better understanding arrive.

You can start from a question that interests you, or follow the drafts from the beginning and watch how these ideas grew step by step.

- [Theory framework](./framework/index.md): how the ideas hold together.
- [Research methods](./framework/methods/index.md): how to ask questions, find evidence, and check and correct your own understanding.
- [Theory applications](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site-en/applications/): putting the ideas to work on concrete problems.
- [Reading the drafts](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site-en/framework/book/): read on in the order of the five parts and fifty-two chapters.

## English-first public research

English is now the primary public edition. Chinese can remain the drafting language; corresponding versions preserve the same claims, assumptions and article identifiers. The current collection is available in English in full.

Corrections, counterexamples and translation reports are welcome through [Research feedback](https://github.com/sl-knowledge/knowledge-site-en/issues/new?template=research-feedback.yml). Include the article version and the precise statement you are questioning.
