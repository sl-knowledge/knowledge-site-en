This section collects research methods that work across domains, and the architecture that keeps evidence, prediction and revision running. Shared definitions are in the [canonical vocabulary](./canonical-vocabulary.md); the position of the whole body of work is in the [theory framework](../index.md).

<a id="representation"></a>

## Building representations

- [CF Grammar: From Constraint Diagnosis to a Testable Research Representation](./cf-grammar.md): organises candidate variables, mechanisms and unknowns through system, target, environment and horizon; a questioning scaffold and a diagnostic method.

<a id="argument-analysis"></a>

## Argument and disagreement analysis

- [The Structure and Generative Mechanisms of Ethical Disagreement](./ethical-disagreement-map.md): connect facts, roles, evaluation rules and feasible plans through H0–H3, with a workflow, recording template and worked cases.

<a id="testing"></a>

## Discrimination and scenarios

- [Stress Testing: Constraint Identification, Feedback and Recovery Windows](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site-en/framework/methods/stress-testing): the ST1–ST12 principles, checking scenarios, feedback, withdrawal of support, recovery deadlines and common failure.

<a id="research-system"></a>

## Human-AI collaboration and long-running research

- [Human-AI Joint Cognitive Research System](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site-en/framework/book/human-ai-research-system): the fifth of the five drafts, on maintaining representations, evidence, predictions, versions and revision records over the long run.

- [From a Concrete Question to a Generative Mechanism: A Human–AI Research Process](./from-question-to-mechanism.md): a brief case showing six steps from a concrete question to a mechanism draft.

- [From an Ethical Debate to a Map of Argumentative Disagreement: A Human–AI Research Case](./from-ethical-debate-to-argument-map.md): a case of reframing ethical debate through representation, agent roles and scope control.

<a id="validation"></a>

## Validation and revision

- [Originality Self-Check and Independent Validation Plan](./originality-and-validation.md): turns candidate ideas into comparison questions with baselines, ablations and prospective records.
- [From Research Cards to Drafts: Consistency Audit and Revision Notes](./consistency-and-revision.md): the conceptual corrections and the division of definitions across the parts.

## Entering a concrete domain

The charters for company analysis and investment research sit in [theory applications → company and investment](https://sl-knowledge.github.io/knowledge-site-en/applications/#company). They turn general methods into domain research with financial, competitive, price and cash-cycle conditions.
