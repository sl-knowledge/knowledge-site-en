> **Public edition v0.2 · A business-mechanism case.** Organised from the original memorandum's first six sections, retaining the research baseline of 9 October 2026; public editing dated 1 November 2026. Financial definitions follow the 2026 interim report, and mechanisms are discussed within that material window.

## 1. Identify the change under study

Separate three questions in a transforming company: has business organisation changed; does that change improve cash/capital efficiency; and can effective practices transfer into new markets?

For Oriental Yuhong, the original research examines increasing retail and engineering-channel weights from an earlier project/large-customer-dependent model, and whether this reduces credit capital tied up behind revenue.

"Business mechanism" means the connections among products, channels, delivery, credit and collection. "Reconstruction capability" means rearranging them after environmental change and correcting ineffective changes promptly.

## 2. Channel change: what is established?

The 2026 interim report discloses combined engineering/retail-channel revenue of RMB12.723 billion, 85.58% of operating revenue. This confirms importance and structure, not improved long-term credit losses or capital use. [Interim report, p.10](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-08-20/8c65816d-5eb3-484c-b7df-a7f54137d618.PDF#page=10)

The mechanism hypothesis is that dispersed customer links, sales service and credit management may change revenue-related capital use. Check who bears risk, company guarantees, distributor inventory and whether sales become cash.

The discriminating question is: **under comparable revenue/business conditions, how much capital do new channels require, what eventual credit losses arise, and what sustainable cash remains?**

## 3. Genuine improvement or reporting appearance?

Decompose lower receivables and better operating cash flow.

| Observation | Possible operating improvement | Alternatives to examine |
|---|---|---|
| Lower receivables | Timelier payment/better collection | Lower revenue, write-offs, impairment, asset transfers |
| Better operating cash flow | Faster cash conversion | Delayed supplier payment, less purchasing, one-off working-capital release |
| Higher channel revenue share | Different customer/credit organisation | Old-business contraction or incomplete risk transfer |
| Higher margin | Product/channel/operational efficiency | Raw materials, mix, expense timing |

Explanations can coexist. Align seasonality, revenue scale, contract structure and accounting. Group figures do not establish independent channel credit quality.

## 4. What can historical growth establish?

The official history records regional waterproofing construction beginning in 1995, a 2008 listing and major projects. These are clues to delivery and organisational expansion. [Initial stage](https://www.yuhong.com.cn/about/history/Initial/), [2004–2009](https://www.yuhong.com.cn/about/history/Second/)

Distinguish:

- Delivering projects from collecting high-quality cash;
- Replicating channels nationwide from earning good incremental returns in every market;
- Rapid growth with rising demand from anticipating cross-cycle credit risks;
- Announced rule changes from independently verified performance.

Past rapid growth is a starting point, not the conclusion. Examine loss control and new paths during expansion, contraction and environmental change.

## 5. Overseas: does capability transfer?

The interim report places Chilean building-material retailer CONSTRUMART in the company's brand/business map, creating an observation window. [Report, p.12](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-08-20/8c65816d-5eb3-484c-b7df-a7f54137d618.PDF#page=12)

Two proposed paths remain hypotheses: apply procurement, inventory, warehousing/distribution and management experience locally; connect new products through existing channels.

Transferring domestic manufacturing, project logistics and national channels to overseas retail reopens assortment, local management, demand and institutional questions. Resources are only a start; connecting them effectively in the new environment is actual transfer.

Test acquired-business operating profit, cash returns, inventory and new investment. Own products entering stores are supporting evidence, not a substitute for total capital returns.

## 6. Questions for the next review

1. **Domestic channels:** comparable receivables, losses, guarantees and inventory risks declining? What remains unknown without channel disclosure?
2. **Collection:** recurring sales receipts or contraction, delayed payment and one-offs?
3. **Overseas:** more operating profit/cash with controlled incremental capital?
4. **Allocation:** new returns compensate risk/opportunity cost? Can ineffective projects stop, exit or correct?
5. **Alternatives:** after materials, demand and credit conditions explain their share, how much independent reconstruction evidence remains?

## 7. Interim conclusion and falsification

Disclosures support channel change. Long-term credit-capital improvement and overseas transfer still need evidence.

Weakening observations: continued heavy channel credit use; cash gains mainly from delayed payment; overseas growth requiring continual capital without returns; failure to correct inefficient projects.

Stronger support: jointly better collection, credit losses and cash per unit capital under comparable revenue/environment, with an account of their sources.

Retain the method of testing "organisation changed", "operating quality improved" and "capability replicates" separately. Evidence must connect their causal links.
